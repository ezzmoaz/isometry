Privacy policy

Isometry · effective 22 October 2026

Isometry does not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Game progress and settings

Your finished levels, their marks and your settings are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them completely.

The hinge on iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo the game reads the angle of the phone's hinge while you play, to fold the flap you have chosen. The angle is used only on your phone, at that moment. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code or services, so no other company receives any data from it.
