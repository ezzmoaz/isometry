Privacy policy

Isometry · effective 22 October 2026

Isometry does not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The only time it connects to the internet is to ask the App Store whether a newer version of Isometry is out.

Game progress and settings

Your finished levels, their marks and your settings are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them completely.

The hinge on iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo the game reads the angle of the phone's hinge while you play, to fold the flap you have chosen. The angle is used only on your phone, at that moment. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.

Checking for updates

Each time you open Isometry, it asks Apple's public App Store lookup service which version of Isometry is current. The request names the app and the store country, and nothing about you. Like any internet request it reaches Apple from your network address, which the app itself never sees or keeps. If a newer version is out, the game asks you to update it from the App Store. Offline, the game simply plays on.

Ratings

After you finish a chapter the game may show Apple's own rating window, and the home page has a link to write a review on the App Store. Whatever you choose happens with Apple; the app learns nothing about it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app includes one open-source library, Siren, which makes the update check above. Its only request goes to Apple; nothing is sent to its authors or to any other company.
